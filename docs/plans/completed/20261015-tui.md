# TUI

`cctrail` should show the numbers lightly: open, look, drill into a project or a model, close. A
ratatui interface on the approved mockup: projects, models and a timeline, each with a drill-down,
over the history parsed once.

## Decisions

- `cctrail` with no arguments and a terminal on stdout opens the TUI; any argument, `--json` or a
  pipe keeps the text report.
- The history is parsed once behind a loading frame; switching period, provider, sort or view is
  in memory. Numbers come from the existing accounting (`stats_by`, `usage_by_period`, `pricing`):
  a period and provider show the same totals as the text report.
- Periods: today, this week, this month, the last 30 days, all. Provider: both, claude, codex
  (events filtered by provider, the folds computed on all of them).
- Views: 1 Projects (time, tokens, cost, sessions, share; every project, `other` stays in the text
  report) → the project's models (time per model) and its paths; 2 Models (one row per model,
  fast and standard together) → its use by speed and by effort (as the logs record it: Claude's
  `perTurnEffort` or `effort`, Codex's `turn_context` effort); 3 Timeline (a chart over the top
  half, a bar per day or per week for all, stacked by model with a legend; the days as a table
  under it) → the projects of that day.
- Boxes as macmon's (its border helpers): a header box with the views and the version on its
  border, the periods and the provider inside; the list's box with its name and count, the
  filter, `sort` and `→ open` on its top border, the paths or the unpriced note and the key hints
  on its bottom one, the scrollbar on its right one; on the timeline a chart box over it. All
  choices clickable.
- Keys and lists as macmon's: t / T period (wraps), ↑↓ select (wraps), → / enter open, ← / esc /
  ⌫ / q back, PgUp/PgDn and Home/End, the wheel 3 rows (wraps; queued wheel events folded into
  one step at most every 30 ms), s next sort column and S reverse (the share is of the sorted
  column; on the timeline `s` picks the bars: time, tokens, cost), p provider, / filter, ? help,
  q quit. Only a left button press clicks.
- ratatui with only its crossterm backend (no default features).

## Result

`cctrail` on a terminal opens the interactive view; the text report and JSON are unchanged. The
totals equal the text report's for the same period and provider. On the full local history the
first frame waits for the usual parse (~4.5 s); a switch or drill-down then takes ~10 ms. The
module is ~870 lines with its tests.
